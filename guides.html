F2P Raid: Shadow Legends Guides & Strategy Series

Every series, every guide — organized by level and topic. No fluff, no filler. Real strategy for real players.

Every guide on this channel is built around one rule: if it doesn't work for a free-to-play player, it doesn't get made. That means no tier-list copying, no pay-to-win shortcuts, and no advice that only applies if you've already spent hundreds of dollars on the game. Real strategy for real accounts — beginner through endgame.

The content library covers the full progression path. The F2P Files series documents a fresh account from day one, walking through every decision a new player faces and why. The R.A.I.D. Gear Formula series gives you a repeatable four-step system for gearing any champion correctly without guesswork. Account Reviews show real community rosters being diagnosed live — follow the logic and apply it to your own account.

For endgame players, the DuesXMac account covers Clan Boss, Hydra, Chimera, and Dungeons in depth. Free cheat sheets and gear reference guides are available on Ko-fi, and the Discord is the best place to ask questions between videos.

Signature Framework

The R.A.I.D. Gear Formula

The four-step system behind every gear decision on this channel. Learn it once, apply it forever.

R
Role
What job does this champion do? Damage, support, tank, control? Define it before touching gear.
A
Aim
What specific stat benchmarks does this role require to actually function? Set clear targets.
I
Identify
Search your vault for gear that gets you closest to those targets. Work with what you have.
D
Dial In
Fine-tune with accessories and masteries. Test in content. Adjust. Repeat until it works.
