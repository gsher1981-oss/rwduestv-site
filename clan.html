Join the UNG Cluster — Raid: Shadow Legends Community

14 clans. All levels welcome. United in Battle, United in Diversity — find your home and raid with purpose.

Apply to a Clan Join the Discord

The UNG Cluster is a 14-clan community built by and for Raid: Shadow Legends players who want a real home in the game. The cluster is run by fellow content creator Boozor and spans every level of play, from brand-new players finding their feet to endgame accounts pushing the hardest content in the game. Every clan in the cluster runs on the same values: honest play, active participation, and community first.

I'm part of the UNG Cluster community and I'm happy to point you in the right direction. Whether you're just starting out or you've been playing for years, there's a clan in the cluster that fits where you're at right now.

Apply through the form or jump into the UNG Discord and tell them where you're at — they'll get you placed in the right spot and help you chart a path forward from there.
