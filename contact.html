Apply to Join the UNG Cluster — Find Your Raid Clan

All levels welcome. Fill out the form and we'll match you to the right clan in the UNG Cluster — or reach out directly on Discord.

The UNG Cluster is a 14-clan community in Raid: Shadow Legends built for players who want a real, active home in the game — not just a clan to park an account in. Every level of player is welcome. Whether you're just starting out and looking for a supportive environment to learn in, or you're a seasoned player ready for a more competitive setting, there's a clan in the cluster that fits where you're at right now.

The UNG Cluster has a clan for every level of play — from players just starting out all the way to endgame. If you're not sure which clan fits you best, just say so on the form and the team will place you in the right spot based on your account and goals.

The fastest way to get sorted is always Discord — jump into the RWD server or the UNG Cluster server and someone from the team will get you squared away. The application form below works just as well and goes to the same people. Most applications get a response within 24–48 hours.
