Raiding With Dues is a Raid: Shadow Legends YouTube channel built around one idea: honest strategy that works for everyone, not just players with deep pockets. Every guide comes from an active F2P account and a separate endgame account — so whether you're figuring out your first champion build or pushing Hydra Clan Boss, the advice here is grounded in real gameplay.

The channel launched in 2020 and covers the full progression journey — beginner gear decisions, F2P account building from scratch, endgame content like Clan Boss and Dungeons, fusion guides, and free reward codes. The signature R.A.I.D. Gear Formula gives players a four-step system for making every gear decision with confidence instead of copying tier lists and hoping for the best.

Beyond the videos, Dues is part of the UNG Cluster — a 14-clan community run by fellow content creator Boozor. Free F2P checklists and cheat sheets are available on Ko-fi, and the Discord is open to anyone who wants to talk strategy, get an account review, or just find a good clan.
